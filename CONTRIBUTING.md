# Contributing to LGMP

Issue reports and technical discussions are welcome. Open an issue before
preparing a patch.

LGMP is not currently accepting unsolicited contributions to source code,
documentation, tests, build files, continuous-integration configuration, or
other project assets. LGMP may be made available under separate license terms,
so accepted contributions must preserve the project's ability to offer those
terms.

Before an invited contribution can be accepted, the contributor—and their
employer, where applicable—must sign the contribution agreement designated by
the maintainer. That agreement must grant the rights needed to use and
sublicense the contribution under both the public GPL license and separate
license terms. A Developer Certificate of Origin sign-off or `Signed-off-by`
line is not a substitute for that agreement.
